# Vision: the Ultimate pack

## Pitch (owner, 2026-11-24)
"We are going to unify all the mods into an ultimate modpack basically which balances and extends them together too along with calamity and thorium." `owner`

## Must-haves (to confirm with the owner)
1. One pack in which Khemet, Olympia, The Wilds Beyond, Calamity and Thorium run together. `owner`
2. Our content is **balanced** against Calamity's and Thorium's progression, by data, not by feel. `owner`
3. Our content **extends** them: our bosses and events join their progression order and boss checklists; our guild contracts can ask for their creatures and items. `owner`
4. Nothing of Calamity or Thorium is copied or redistributed. `licence`
