# Design

## Components
1. **Content modules**: `terraria-khemet`, `terraria-olmypia`, `terraria-wilds` stay the sources of truth (content as data, generated by SHADW).
2. **`Ultimate` (generated)**: the SHADW tool gains a `merge` step that combines module content into one mod, namespacing each module's classes and ids so nothing collides. One build, one progression, one balance table.
3. **Compat layer** (weak references; the pack works with either mod absent): `ModLoader.TryGetMod("CalamityMod")` and `"ThoriumMod"`, `Mod.Call` where they expose it (Calamity has `ModSupport/ModCalls`), and **Boss Checklist** registration for our bosses.
4. **Balance layer**: `balance/*.json` targets by tier. Applied to **our** content (at generation time, in each module's `items.json`/`npcs.json`) and never to theirs.
5. **Progression map**: each of our bosses and gated items is placed on one shared ladder (vanilla milestones, Calamity's order, Thorium's order), recorded in data.

## Method for balance (what exists now)
`extract_power_curve.py` -> `balance/<mod>-power-curve.json` -> `balance_report.py`. Bands are the reference mod's own p25..p75 at a rarity. Known weaknesses: rarity is a proxy, not a progression stage; Calamity's own rarities (Turquoise, PureGreen ...) are not vanilla ones, so late tiers have no vanilla anchor; boss life is mostly computed in code, not literal, so it needs a runtime export or the wiki.
